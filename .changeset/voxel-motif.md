Add the `VoxelMotif` module and a `LauncherCard` media slot. `VoxelMotif` renders the six approved per-product voxel landmarks (home lighthouse, photos tree/windmill, yang beacon, trict chart, twinkle fireflies) as one scalable SVG, ported from the redesign-2026 mockup geometry; `LauncherCard` gains an optional `media` block above the icon/label row for motif thumbnails.
